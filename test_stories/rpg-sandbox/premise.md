# Shadows of Thornwood

## Premise
A dark fantasy RPG where the player awakens in the village of Thornwood, nestled
at the edge of a cursed forest. Strange things have been happening: livestock
vanishing, travelers disappearing on the forest road, and eerie lights seen
deep in the woods at night. The village elder, Mara, has called for help.

## Tone
Dark fantasy with moments of warmth. Think Witcher meets classic text adventure.

## Themes
- Courage vs. caution
- Trust and deception
- The cost of power
- Nature corrupted vs. nature healed

## World Overview
The village of Thornwood sits at the crossroads of the Thornwood Forest and
the old King's Road. North lies the ruined Thornwood Keep, abandoned after a
magical catastrophe fifty years ago. The forest itself is ancient, dotted with
sacred groves and forgotten shrines.

## Opening Situation
The player arrives in Thornwood at dusk, responding to Elder Mara's call for
aid. The village is tense, the people fearful. The first night, strange howls
echo from the forest.
