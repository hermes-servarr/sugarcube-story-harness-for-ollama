# Jack

A ruggedly handsome man in his late 30s with a mysterious aura around him. Jack is known to be a skilled private investigator who can dig up dirt on anyone, but he also has his own secrets buried deep within the city's underbelly.Jack takes an interest in Sophia's plight and offers his services for her revenge mission. the two soon form a bond over their mutual hatred of deception.
