# King Atlantas

Once a mighty ruler of Atlantis, King Atlantas has grown weary from the endless struggles that have plagued his reign. As he faces new challenges to his rule and grapples with maintaining order amidst growing dissent among his people, he must confront the possibility that his once-great city may be beyond saving.
