# Warden Kaelyn

As the head of security for Atlantis, Warden Kaelyn is responsible for maintaining order and protecting its inhabitants from dangers both within and outside their realm. Known for their keen intuition and strategic prowess, they must navigate the complex web of alliances and betrayals that have come to define this ancient city.
