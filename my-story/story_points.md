# Story Points

## The Discovery of the Map

- You find an ancient map hidden away in a forgotten corner of your research.
- Deciphering it, you learn that it leads to the lost city of Atlantis.

## The Journey to Atlantis

- Embarking on a perilous journey across treacherous waters, you face numerous challenges and dangers along the way.
- Finally reaching Atlantis, its ruins and secrets unveiled before your eyes.

## Uncovering Atlantis\'s Secrets

- Exploring the city's remains, you uncover lost knowledge of ancient technology and powerful artifacts.
- As you delve deeper into the mysteries surrounding this once-great civilization, a choice presents itself - to reclaim their knowledge or leave it buried with them forever.

## Open Questions

1. The dangers faced during your journey to Atlantis.
2. What secrets did Atlantis hold that are still undiscovered?
3. How will the decision to either reclaim Atlantis's lost knowledge or leave it buried impact the world?
