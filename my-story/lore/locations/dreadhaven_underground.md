# The Ravenhold Market

Nestled beneath the city's grimy streets, The Ravenhold Market is a hidden gem where shadows dance and secrets unfold. Stalls brimming with illicit goods are run by an eclectic mix of vendors; from smugglers to thieves, all seeking to make a quick fortune. The air here reeks of danger, but it also carries the enticing aroma of opportunity.
