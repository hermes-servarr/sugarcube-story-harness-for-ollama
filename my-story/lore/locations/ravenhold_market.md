# Ravenhold Market

Nestled in the heart of Atlantis, Ravenhold Market is a bustling hub of commerce where merchants peddle their wares to curious adventurers and locals alike. The air is thick with the scent of exotic spices and silken fabrics as traders haggle over prices and stories are exchanged about the mythical city's past. As you wander through its crowded lanes, be prepared to barter and discover hidden treasures among its stalls.
