# The Neon Maze

A labyrinthine web of neon lights strung across the city's skyline, The Neon Maze is a beacon of hope in Tenebrous' perpetual twilight. Its glowing arteries lead to the most sought-after locations and events; from clandestine nightclubs to exclusive rooftop parties. But beware, for amidst the dazzling spectacle lies danger: those who venture too far into the maze risk becoming lost forever.
